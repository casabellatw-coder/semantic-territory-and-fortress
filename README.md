## Scope note

The English words "semantic territory" and "semantic fortress" have appeared in other contexts before. This record does not claim the words. It documents Nelson Chou's paired definitions of the two terms, first verifiably published on NelsonChou.com on 10 July 2026, together with their three-layer relationship to Semantic Sovereignty.

## Definitions

**Semantic Territory (語意領地)** — the digital ground you actually own and can compound over time — bounded by your own domain and the structures you control, indifferent to platform rules.

**Semantic Fortress (語意堡壘)** — the self-defining structure built on your semantic territory, so that people, search engines, and AI arrive at one consistent understanding of who you are — hard to distort, contaminate, or collage.

## Three-layer relationship

Semantic Territory (the ground) → Semantic Fortress (the building) → Semantic Sovereignty (the state you hold as a result)

Semantic Sovereignty is recorded here only as the resulting state named by the primary definition page; it is not a third defined term in this record.
